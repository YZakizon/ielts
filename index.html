IELTS Study Hub is built for learners who need practical academic vocabulary practice and fast sentence translation while preparing for IELTS Reading, Writing, Speaking, and Listening. Use the vocabulary builder to learn words by level, then use the sentence translator to compare meaning across languages.

What can I practice here?

You can practice IELTS vocabulary, review synonyms and examples, search for specific academic words, and translate full study sentences.

Which languages are supported?

The sentence translator includes source language detection and target language options including English, Bahasa Indonesia, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

Is this only for one country?

No. The app is designed for IELTS learners in any language background who want clearer vocabulary usage and multilingual sentence support.
